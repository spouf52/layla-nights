## Brand imagery

- `brand/stellar-poster.jpg`: frame extracted at 8 seconds from the organizer-supplied promo MP4. Original promotional lettering retained.
- `brand/stellar-cover-v1.png`: edited version of that still, generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt: “Remove ALL lettering, logos, numbers, text, glowing red writing, and their reflections from the black rock surface and water. Preserve the actual original scene: the floating dark monolith, detailed black chains, midnight blue starry sky and water reflections. Keep the rock dark with subtle red illuminated cracks and red reflections, clean dark negative space across its middle. No added people, objects, typography, lettering, interface or logos.” Used only as the festival background; UI copy is rendered accessibly on top.
- `brand/layla-logo-launch.png`: launch-screen logo isolated from the organizer-provided screenshot `/Users/soufianebrka/Downloads/WhatsApp Image 2026-09-14 at 04.13.47.jpeg`. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using a precise-object-edit prompt to preserve the central LAŸLA Latin/Arabic mark, black planet and red/orange rim while removing the phone UI and blurred background. Final asset is bundled for the five-second launch screen; organizer should confirm final logo usage before release.
- `maps/venue-reference.png`: original organizer-provided Source Marrakech venue plan. This is not the final festival-zone map. Prototype pins are not overlaid on this reference because their positions have not been verified.
